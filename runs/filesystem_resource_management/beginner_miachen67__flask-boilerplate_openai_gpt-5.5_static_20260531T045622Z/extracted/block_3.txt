
## What it checks

The cleanup script detects common generated or temporary project artifacts, including:

- Python bytecode caches such as `__pycache__/`, `*.pyc`, and `*.pyo`
- test and lint caches such as `.pytest_cache/`, `.mypy_cache/`, and `.ruff_cache/`
- coverage/build artifacts such as `.coverage`, `htmlcov/`, `build/`, and `dist/`
- editor/OS leftovers such as `.DS_Store` and backup files ending in `~`
- text files with trailing whitespace
- text files missing a final newline
